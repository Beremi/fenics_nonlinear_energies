## Status vocabulary

- **Corrected in working manuscript/code:** the stated response is present in the
  current working tree, subject to the verification named in the row.
- **Partially corrected:** the principal response is present, but at least one
  synchronization, test, or wording item remains.
- **Implementation in progress:** code or a contract is being added or revised;
  the row is not closed until its tests and dry-run checks pass.
- **Open evidence gate:** no textual or local implementation change can close the
  item; a clean committed execution, external action, or release artifact is
  required.
- **Scope decision:** the finding is addressed by narrowing the venue or claim,
  not by asserting stronger evidence.

## Required closure sequence

The rows above are not independent checkboxes. The release order is:

1. Preserve the passing local safety, source-freeze, environment, factor,
   discretization, scaling, authorization, and accounting tests; make no further
   protocol change without a new version and hash refresh.
2. Freeze one clean reviewed commit and regenerate the matrix, static manifests,
   handoff hashes, protocols, and dry-run plans from that commit.
3. Obtain explicit allocation/account/QoS revalidation and separate human
   authorization for each permitted tranche. Preparation alone is not
   authorization.
4. Execute only the authorized Karolina tranches. Retain failures and censors;
   do not change policies after inspecting holdout timings.
5. Run the strict route, endpoint, discretization, and scaling analyzers. Promote
   only records that pass their complete correctness, accuracy, provenance, and
   uncertainty gates.
6. Update manuscript tables and claims from the admitted manifest, perform a
   fresh internal independent-style review, and obtain genuine external peer
   review through the journal process.
7. Deposit an immutable release archive and verify its persistent identifier
   before changing the availability statement.

Until steps 1–7 are complete, the scientifically accurate disposition is:
mathematical statements substantially corrected, local diagnostic verification
informative but not publication-grade, Karolina execution prepared but not run,
no route-ranking or scaling claim, no external sign-off, and SIOPT out of scope.
