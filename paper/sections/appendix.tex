\subsection{Reference-Formula and Frozen-PMG Diagnostics for 3D Mohr--Coulomb}

In \Cref{sec:num-plasticity3d}, the primary 3D plasticity configuration uses
rank-local constitutive AD.
\Cref{tab:plasticity3d-constitutive-reference-formula,tab:plasticity3d-frozen-preconditioner-pmg}
add two comparisons on the same $P_4(L_2)$ problem from
\eqref{eq:plasticity3d-discrete} at $\lambda_{\mathrm{sr}}=\num{1.0}$: a
non-AD evaluation of the active branch tangent and a PMG variant with the
multigrid operator held fixed during nonlinear iterations.
Both use Armijo Newton, the gradient stopping metric reported in the tables, at
most 50 Newton iterations, and PETSc relative KSP tolerance \num{1e-1}. Here
reference-formula assembly means
evaluating the active constitutive branch and tangent formulas without AD,
while frozen-preconditioner PMG means a variant in which the multigrid operator
is not rebuilt along the nonlinear path.

These diagnostics support two narrower statements. First,
\Cref{tab:plasticity3d-constitutive-reference-formula} shows lower wall time for
reference-formula branch-tangent assembly at each listed rank count; the
AD/reference wall-time ratio decreases from \num{1.805} at 4 ranks to
\num{1.085} at 32 ranks, and all listed entries satisfy the stated gradient
target. Second, \Cref{tab:plasticity3d-frozen-preconditioner-pmg} shows less
consistent convergence for the frozen-preconditioner PMG variant: three of the
eight configurations reach the 50-Newton cap. The rank-local constitutive-AD
realization remains the primary 3D plasticity configuration because it uses the
same local differentiable formulation as the derivative-route and converged
scaling studies; the reference-formula configurations are retained to expose
solver-policy sensitivity on matched discrete problems.
