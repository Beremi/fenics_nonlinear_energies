These tables support two narrower statements. First, reference-formula assembly

Second, the fixed reference-operator PMG profile is not the default used in the
present study: it may appear favorable on completed low-rank rows, but it hits
the Newton cap on more of the reported rank counts and therefore gives less
consistent convergence evidence. The rank-local constitutive-AD path remains the
primary Plasticity3D realization because it uses the
same local differentiable formulation as the derivative-route and converged
scaling studies; the reference-formula rows are retained to expose
solver-policy sensitivity on matched discrete problems.
